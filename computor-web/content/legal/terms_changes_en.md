# What changes

Terms of Use version 1.6, valid from {{TERMS_EFFECTIVE_FROM}}

For accounts created before version 1.6 was published,
[version 1.6](/terms/en) replaces the previous version (1.0 to 1.5) from
{{TERMS_EFFECTIVE_FROM}} automatically. Compared with version 1.0:

- **Sign-in** with email and password instead of GitHub (section 1).
- **Additional services:** learning aids including AI (Lena through “Ask Lena” and
  in course messages, in configured courses also by automatic routing), working
  with tools on your device, and public learning material without an account
  (section 2).
- **More allowed:** network services, proxies and security exercises where the
  course or platform provides them (section 3).
- **Clarifications:** workspaces stop after about one hour without activity;
  technical access and security events are logged for secure operation; you do
  not take over TU Graz's legal obligations; the Privacy notice informs and needs
  no consent (sections 2, 3, 6, 8).
- **Unchanged:** at least 30 days' notice by email before substantial
  restrictions, closure, termination by us, deletion of inactive accounts and
  changes to these terms; changes to your disadvantage apply only with your
  consent (sections 5, 8).

If you disagree, you may end the contract at any time and request deletion of
your account at privacy@computor.at. For questions, please contact contact@computor.at. The
[Privacy notice 1.6](/privacy/en) is published at the same time. The German
version is authoritative.
