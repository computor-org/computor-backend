# What changes

Terms of Use version 1.8, dated 30 October 2026

[Version 1.8](/terms/en) applies to new usage contracts from its publication.
It applies to an existing usage contract only once you expressly agree to it;
until then the previous version applies to you. If you do not agree within the
period stated in section 8, your contract may end. Compared with version 1.7:

- **Ways of working and processing locations:** Depending on the feature
  offered, workspaces, exercises, tests and AI learning aids such as Lena may
  run on your device, including entirely in the browser, on TU Graz systems,
  on our servers at Hetzner, or in any combination of these. The current
  offering determines what is supported (section 2).
- **Local and server-side data:** The Privacy notice distinguishes local
  computation and storage from upload, sync, submission and server-side
  processing. It explains which retention rules and safeguards apply to our
  systems. Stopping inactive workspaces while keeping their files applies to
  workspaces we host (section 2).
- **Your content:** The licence, limited to operating the service, expressly
  covers making your content available to your course's teachers and tutors;
  its purpose and audience limits remain in place (section 3).

Other wording has been revised without changing deletion and appeal rights or
the rules for contractual changes.

If you disagree, you may end the contract at any time and request deletion of
your account at privacy@computor.at. For questions, please contact
contact@computor.at. The [Privacy notice 1.8](/privacy/en) is published at the
same time. The German
version is authoritative.
