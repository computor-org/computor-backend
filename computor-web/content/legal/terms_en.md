availability or recovery of individual files. Back up important work through Git

Tests and points are learning aids, not an examination certificate or proof of

## 4. Moderation and appeals

We act on specific indications of legal or usage breaches; use [Report content](/report) to report illegal content. Measures
depend on severity: a warning, removing or disabling content, stopping a workspace,
temporary suspension of the account or termination. As a rule, a human decides
on measures against you. Exceptions: where there is acute danger or obvious
abuse, such as mass registrations, spam, attacks or resource abuse, automated
tools may take provisional measures. Such a measure stays in place until we lift
it or a human has decided on it. If you ask for a review by email to
contact@computor.at, a human reviews the measure without undue delay; your
request also counts as an appeal under the next paragraph.

We explain by email the measure, facts, rules concerned, use of reports or
automated detection, and how you can challenge it, unless law or an authority
prohibits this (Art. 17 DSA). You may appeal to contact@computor.at within six
months. A human decides on the appeal; we then give you a reasoned outcome. You
may also go to court at any time. If we suspect a criminal offence threatening
people's life or safety, we promptly inform the competent authorities (Art. 18
DSA).
