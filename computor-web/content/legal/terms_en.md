# Terms of Use computor.at

Version 1.1, effective on publication of public Luna

This is a translation. The German version (Nutzungsbedingungen) is
authoritative.

## Summary

This summary makes reading easier. The full text below is binding.

- computor.at is a free learning service of TU Graz, funded by netidee.
- Your code stays yours. We use it only to provide the service.
- The workspace is for learning: no mining, no attacks, no scans, no spam,
  nothing illegal.
- Resources and course places are limited. Keep copies of important work via
  Git or by downloading it.
- We announce material changes at least 30 days in advance; changes to your
  detriment apply only once you agree to them.
- Austrian law; your mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

## 1. Provider and scope

1.1 The learning platform computor.at and the chat chat.computor.at (together
the “platform”) are operated by Graz University of Technology,
Rechbauerstraße 12, 8010 Graz, Austria (“TU Graz” or “we”), through its
Institute of Theoretical Physics – Computational Physics (ITPcp),
Petersgasse 16, 8010 Graz. Details are in the [Imprint](/impressum).

1.2 These Terms of Use govern the use of the platform by registered users
(“you”). How we process your data is explained in the
[Privacy notice](/privacy).

1.3 If you use the platform as part of a TU Graz course, TU Graz's study
regulations and the requirements of that course also apply. In case of
conflict, they take precedence.

## 2. Account and conclusion of contract

2.1 You sign in with your GitHub account. At your first sign-in you confirm
that you accept these Terms of Use. This concludes a free-of-charge usage
contract for an indefinite period. You can also use the chat
chat.computor.at with a separate chat account; these Terms of Use apply to it
as well.

2.2 You must be at least 14 years old. Use is free of charge and creates no
payment obligations for you; young people aged 14 and over can therefore agree
to it themselves (§ 170(3) Austrian Civil Code, ABGB). We delete accounts of
persons under 14 as soon as we become aware of them.

2.3 Each person may have only one account. Accounts are not transferable.
Protect access to your GitHub account. If you suspect that someone else is
using your account, please report it immediately to security@computor.at.

## 3. Services

3.1 We provide free of charge: courses and exercises, a programming
environment in the browser (“workspace”), automatic testing of your
submissions, Git repositories for your submissions, a chat, and Luna tutoring
subject to capacity limits.

3.2 We operate the platform with the care customary for a university learning
service. Where possible, we announce maintenance on the platform in advance.
We cannot promise uninterrupted availability.

3.3 Computing time, memory, storage, network access and the number and running
time of workspaces are limited. Workspaces stop automatically after one hour
without activity; your files are kept. Course places and the number of
workspaces that can run at the same time may be limited. When capacity is
exhausted, enrolling in a course or starting a workspace may temporarily not
be possible. There is no entitlement to a particular course place.

3.4 We regularly back up the servers but cannot promise to restore individual
files. Please keep copies of important work yourself by submitting it via Git
or downloading it.

3.5 Automatic tests and points are learning aids. They are not an exam, a
certificate or a confirmation of competence. If a university course uses
results for an assessment, the responsible teacher decides under the
applicable regulations.

3.6 When you ask Luna, your question and relevant submitted work are processed
on TU Graz-operated inference servers to generate a learning aid. Luna can be
wrong; its answer is not an official assessment. Processing and storage are
explained in the [Privacy notice](/privacy).

## 4. Changes to and discontinuation of services

4.1 We may change the platform where this is reasonable for you, in particular
because the change is minor and objectively justified. Objective reasons
include technical development, platform security, legal requirements or
changed resources for operation.

4.2 We announce material restrictions (e.g. removal of a course you are
enrolled in, or of the workspace service) and the discontinuation of the
platform at least 30 days in advance by email and on the platform, stating the
reasons. During this period you can download your data or request it from us.

4.3 In case of acute danger to the security of the platform, its users or
third parties, we may restrict functions immediately. We then inform you as
soon as possible of the reason and expected duration.

## 5. Acceptable use

5.1 The platform is for learning and teaching programming. In particular, the
following is not permitted:

a) cryptocurrency mining and other compute-intensive use unrelated to a course;
b) attacks on systems, whether against the platform or third parties: port and
   vulnerability scans, denial of service, brute-force attacks, attempts to
   escape the workspace, access to other people's accounts or data;
c) circumventing resource limits, access restrictions or participation limits,
   e.g. by using multiple accounts or automated sign-ups;
d) sending spam, running proxies, VPN or Tor nodes or bots from the workspace,
   and offering publicly reachable services from the workspace;
e) storing, distributing or linking illegal content, e.g. malware for
   distribution, copyright-infringing material, child sexual abuse material,
   incitement to hatred or threats;
f) harassing, insulting or exposing other people;
g) publishing solutions to graded exercises where the course prohibits this,
   or other people's submissions without their consent.

5.2 Security research on the platform is only permitted with prior agreement
via security@computor.at. Please report vulnerabilities to
security@computor.at or confidentially via
https://github.com/computor-org/feedback/security/advisories/new. We will not
take legal action because of the report against anyone who reports a
vulnerability in good faith and confidentially, without accessing other
people's data or disrupting operations.

5.3 To protect the platform, we monitor workspace resource usage automatically
(see [Privacy notice](/privacy)). There is no general monitoring of content.

## 6. Your content

6.1 Code, texts and files you create remain yours. You grant TU Graz only the

free of charge, non-exclusive and ends once the content is deleted; copies in
backups are deleted within the period stated in the privacy notice.

6.2 Upload only content for which you hold the necessary rights.

## 8. Content moderation

8.1 We do not generally review content. We act when we receive indications of
illegal content or of breaches of section 5, for example through a report (see
[Report content](/report)) or through automatic resource monitoring.

8.2 Possible measures, graded by severity, are: a notice to you, removing or
disabling individual content, stopping a workspace, temporarily suspending the
account, termination under section 9. Automated tools flag anomalies; a human
decides on measures against you. To protect against acute danger, a workspace
may also be stopped automatically.

8.3 When we take a measure, we tell you by email what we did, the facts we
relied on, whether it was based on a report or on automated detection, which
law or provision of these terms the content breaches, and how you can
challenge it (Art. 17 DSA). We omit this only where authorities or the law
prohibit it.

8.4 You can appeal any measure within six months to contact@computor.at.
Where possible, the appeal is reviewed by a person who was not involved in the
original decision. We tell you the outcome with reasons. You may go to court
at any time.

8.5 If we become aware of information giving rise to a suspicion of a criminal
offence involving a threat to the life or safety of persons, we promptly
inform the police or the competent authorities (Art. 18 DSA).

## 9. Termination and suspension

9.1 You can end the usage contract at any time without giving reasons by
requesting deletion of your account by email to privacy@computor.at from the
address linked to your account.

9.2 We can terminate the usage contract for objective reasons, e.g. if the
platform is discontinued, with 30 days' notice by email. We delete accounts
that have not been signed in to for 24 months after announcing it by email at
least 30 days in advance; signing in during this period keeps the account.

9.3 In case of serious breaches of section 5 or of the law, or breaches
repeated despite a notice, we can temporarily suspend the account or terminate
the contract without notice. Sections 8.3 and 8.4 apply accordingly.
